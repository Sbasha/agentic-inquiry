Every taxonomy leaf this corpus does not carry, with why it is absent and
what would admit it. A leaf appears here or in the topic set, never in
both and never in neither. This register is not itself a topic and answers
no request; it records the shape of what is missing so a reader can tell an
unevidenced subject from an overlooked one.

## inline-and-progressive-reference-skills

**Why absent.** Authored, then withdrawn: measured retrieval could not
separate it from instruction density and progressive disclosure. Three natural
phrasings of its own subject each selected both.

**What would admit it.** A body whose subject is not already named in another
topic's routing signals, selected alone by at least two measured prompts.

## multimode-artifact-and-script-backed-skills

**Why absent.** Cross-pack source examples combine explicit user-visible modes,
artifact production and packaged scripts. This corpus has no independently
evaluated topic for that combined pattern. Source coverage alone does not
establish that a separate topic is distinguishable from the admitted guidance.

**What would admit it.** A body grounded in the cross-pack examples whose
combined subject is selected alone by at least two measured prompts.

## routers-composed-workflows-and-lifecycle-skills

**Why absent.** Cross-pack source examples combine workflow composition, routing
or dispatch, and lifecycle state. This corpus has no independently evaluated
topic for that combined pattern. The examples establish source coverage, not
that a separate topic is distinguishable from the admitted guidance.

**What would admit it.** A body grounded in the cross-pack examples whose
combined subject is selected alone by at least two measured prompts.

## compatibility-and-runtime-package-patterns

**Why absent.** No body can be written without advertising a packaging mode
this corpus does not offer, which its own boundaries forbid.

**What would admit it.** The runtime profiles that make packaging claims
verifiable, after which the mode's guidance can be stated without advertising
it.

## workspace-orientation-status-and-resumption

**Why absent.** Distinct-pack source examples combine an attention overview,
explicit work state and continuation from persisted context. This corpus has no
independently evaluated topic for their combined practice. Source coverage does
not establish a distinct topic or a doctrine promotion class.

**What would admit it.** A body grounded in the cross-pack examples whose
combined subject is selected alone by at least two measured prompts.

## activation-and-near-miss-evals

**Why absent.** No two observations at distinct skill paths in distinct packs
establish it, and no promotion class is evidenced. Evals exist across packs,
but the near-miss practice itself is not evidenced twice.

**What would admit it.** Two packs whose eval sets are built around declared
near misses.

## behavioral-and-construction-checks

**What would admit it.** Cross-pack evidence treating behavioural and
construction checks as one designed pair.
