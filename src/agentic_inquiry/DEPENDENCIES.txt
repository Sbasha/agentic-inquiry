Agentic Inquiry dependencies and local models

Agentic Inquiry's product code is Apache-2.0. Its distributions do not vendor
the parser libraries, grammars, PDFium binaries or OCR executables. Installation
retains the dependency distributions and their own license files and notices.
Redistribution of a combined environment must retain those notices, including
PDFium's dependency licenses and all bundled grammar licenses.

The grammar bundle is fixed to 0.13.0 for its offline, bundled parser interface.
This release provides CPython 3.10+ ABI wheels for macOS ARM64/x86-64 and Linux
ARM64/x86-64. The activated Python, JavaScript, TypeScript/TSX, Java, Go, Rust,
C, C++, C# and Apex grammars are listed as MIT in the upstream release.
Other bundled grammars are not activated by Agentic Inquiry.

PDF/OCR coordinates identify source page regions or rendered image pixels.
OCR recognition is local and requires a separately available Tesseract binary
and its language data. Agentic Inquiry does not distribute those assets.

Local model assets are downloaded separately and are not included in the wheel
or source distribution. Keep their license files with redistributed caches.
